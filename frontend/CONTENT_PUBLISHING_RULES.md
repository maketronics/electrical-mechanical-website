# Hard Publishing Rules

These rules are mandatory before publishing service pages.

1. No service page links to another service page in the same folder.
   - Cross-links are only allowed across folder types (Mechanical, Electrical, Industry, Approach, Why Us).
2. Title tags must stay under 60 characters.
   - For long keywords (for example, cone crusher structural mounting, mining conveyor system design), keep geo terms out of the title and place geo in meta description.
3. H1 and title tag must not be identical.
   - Title is for CTR; H1 can be longer and descriptive.
4. Case studies must be real or clearly labeled as representative scenarios.
5. Validate each target keyword in Semrush or Ahrefs with country filters:
   - GCC, Indonesia, Vietnam, South Africa
   - If monthly volume is under 50, treat as credibility/support page and prioritize later.
